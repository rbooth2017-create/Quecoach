    Welcome to Cue Coach

    Learn to mix two tracks like a DJ. No controller needed. Where's your music?

    Your level

    Cue Coach grows with you. Higher levels take the help away and add more of the mixer. Switch back any time.

    Appearance

    Pick a look. It's saved for next time.

    AI coach

    Cue Coach can use Claude, Anthropic's AI, to build sets and pick your next track. You need your own Anthropic API key.

    • Get a key at console.anthropic.com. Use costs a few cents per set.
    • Only track details are sent (title, artist, BPM, key, energy, genre). Never your audio or file locations.
    • In the desktop app your key is encrypted with your computer's secure storage.

    Controller and sound

    Cue Coach works with the mouse and keyboard. Plug in a DJ controller and it takes over the decks and mixer.

    Live monitor: touch any control and it shows up here
    Controllers like the DDJ-FLX4 have their own sound card: choose it here and plug your speakers and headphones into the controller.
    • Supported: Pioneer DDJ-FLX4, DDJ-400, DDJ-200, DDJ-SB3; Hercules Inpulse 200, 300, 500; Numark Mixtrack 3. Anything else (like a DDJ-FLX2): plug it in and press Map my controller.
    • Play, cue (back to the start), jog wheels, tempo, EQ, channel faders, crossfader and the headphone buttons (Listen) all work.
    • There's no sync on purpose: matching the speed yourself is the skill you're learning.

    Layout

    Collapse any section with the arrow on its header. Here you can also move sections, or hide the ones you don't use. You can drag a section's header to move it too.

    Settings

    Import a playlist

    Bring a playlist in from Spotify or Apple Music. It becomes a crate: tracks you already have go in, and the rest are listed as still to get, with links to find them. Streamed music can't play on the decks, so you'll need your own copy of each track.

    .csv, .txt or .m3u

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Everything also works with the mouse. A DJ controller is optional.

    QPlay / pause deck A
    PPlay / pause deck B
    ↑ ↓Deck B speed (Shift for bigger steps)
    ← →Nudge deck B, like spinning its wheel
    [ ]Crossfader
    CCheck your beats (Confident level)
    /Search your music
    LShow or hide lessons and your set
    RStart or stop recording
