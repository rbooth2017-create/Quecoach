AI coach

Cue Coach can use Claude, Anthropic's AI, to build sets and pick your next track. You need your own Anthropic API key.

  • Get a key at console.anthropic.com. Use costs a few cents per set.
  • Only track details are sent (title, artist, BPM, key, energy, genre). Never your audio or file locations.
  • In the desktop app your key is encrypted with your computer's secure storage.

Keyboard shortcuts
